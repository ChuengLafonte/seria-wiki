==Description==
Searches all the sprite templates to find a matching sprite.

==Arguments==
{{TemplateArguments
|arg1 = 1
|desc1 = The item/sprite name
}}

==Examples==
<pre>{{ItemIcon|Rotten Flesh}}</pre>
Produces:
{{ItemIcon|Rotten Flesh}}

<pre>{{ItemIcon|iron chestplate}}</pre>
Produces:
{{ItemIcon|iron chestplate}}
[[Category:Icon templates]]
