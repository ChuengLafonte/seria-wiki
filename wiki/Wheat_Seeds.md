'''Wheat Seeds''' are a {{R|C}} item found when harvesting Wheat and can be collected in player islands through Wheat Minions, as well as on Chuville.

Wheat Seeds can be used to replant Wheat on farmland. You can collect them from harvesting fully grown wheat and you can trade them to get Dirt and Clay.

== Collection ==
Collecting Wheat Seeds increases the player's Wheat Seeds [[Collection/List|Collection]], which grants useful items and perks upon collecting certain amounts of Wheat Seeds.
