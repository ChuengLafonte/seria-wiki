'''Wheat Seeds''' are a {{R|C}} item found when harvesting [[Wheat]] and can be collected in player islands through [[Wheat Seeds Minion]]s, as well as on {{Zone|The Barn}} and {{Zone|The Garden}}.

Wheat Seeds can be used to replant Wheat on farmland. You can collect them from harvesting fully grown wheat and you can [[Trades|Trade]] them to get [[Dirt]] and [[Clay]].

== Collection ==
Collecting Wheat Seeds increases the player's Wheat Seeds [[Collections|Collection]], which grants useful items and perks upon collecting certain amounts of Wheat Seeds.
