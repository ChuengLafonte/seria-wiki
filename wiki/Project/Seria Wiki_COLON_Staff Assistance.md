To contact a specific staff member, please use their Message Wall. This is usually the fastest way to get assistance with any problems you might have. It is recommended to read the general guidelines before you ask for assistance.

== Contact Methods ==
If you need to contact a specific wiki staff member, post on their Message Wall. If you need help from an Admin, use the [[Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki:Administrator's Noticeboard|Administrator's Noticeboard]] or post on an Admin's message wall. A staff member's availability may be shown on their user page and on the [[Project:STF|Wiki Staff Page]]. If you have not received a response after one day, try asking another staff member.

For a quicker response, you can contact wiki staff members via the Wiki's Discord server.

== General Guidelines ==
Different members of the staff team specialize on different things. For your best experience, it is recommended to find the most suitable staff member for what you need.
* Contact any active {{sr|rbs}} for help with undoing multiple consecutive edits.
* Contact a {{sr|dm}} to delete a comment or discussion post. If the moderator finds it necessary, they may report it to an {{sr|ad}} to block the offending user.
* Contact {{sr|cms}} if you have accidentally made a page that you feel is unnecessary or have discovered one made by another user. Empty pages also qualify to be deleted.
* Ask {{sr|ces}} or {{sr|ads}} about using templates or other technical matters.
* Contact {{sr|ads}} and {{sr|burs}} to report disruptive users.
