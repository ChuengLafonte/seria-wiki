'''Collections''' are a progressive mechanic where the player collects a certain resource in order to unlock milestones which reward the player with [[Recipe Book|recipes]], [[Trades|item trades]], {{SkyBlock Experience}} and items.

There are two different types of '''Collections''', spilt into sub-sections: resource collections, that are spilt into [[#Farming|Farming]], [[#Mining|Mining]], [[#Combat|Combat]], [[#Foraging|Foraging]] and [[#Fishing|Fishing]] and boss collections that are spilt into each of the [[Dungeons|Dungeon]] {{mcat|boss}} mobs and [[Kuudra]].

The player can view their '''Collections''' through {{CodeTag|[[File:SkyBlock Menu.png|20px|link=SkyBlock Menu]] [[SkyBlock Menu|{{G|SkyBlock Menu}}]] → [[File:Painting.png|20px|link=]] {{G|Collection}}}}. Once the player has collected {{G|25,000}} of a resource, they can see their global ranking for that item. Obtaining items from the [[Bazaar]], [[Merchants]] or [[Trading]] do not count towards '''Collections'''.
